Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

stardust is a research prototype operated by OjbLabs ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use stardust.ojblabs.com (the "site"), why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep collection to the minimum needed to run the experiment.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is OjbLabs, Vakil Whispering Woods, Hosur Main Road, Near Chandapura Circle, Thirumagondanahalli, Marasur, Bangalore, Karnataka 562107, India. For any privacy question or request, contact anil.ojblabs@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us:

  • Your email address and the ticker you ask about when you request a signal or report.
  • The content of any message you send us, including support or privacy requests.
  • Optional responses you choose to give, such as whether the prototype is useful to you.

Information collected automatically:

  • IP address, browser and user-agent information, referring page, and the path you visited.
  • A random visitor identifier, whether you are a returning visitor, and a timestamp.
  • Campaign parameters such as utm_source, utm_medium, and utm_campaign.
  • Prototype usage events, such as a ticker lookup, signal view, call-to-action click, email signup, or survey response, plus limited metadata about that event.
  • Standard server logs used for security and troubleshooting.

Local storage: the site stores a random visitor identifier (sd_vid) and a small unlock flag (sd_unlocked) in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising cookies. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

We do not currently collect payment card details because no paid plan is live. If a paid plan launches, payments will be handled by a payment provider and we will not store your full card details.

3. Why we collect it, and our legal basis

  • To provide the signal, report, and alert you asked for, and to send related service emails. This is based on your consent and on performing the service you requested.
  • To understand whether the prototype is useful and to improve it. This is based on our legitimate interests in researching demand and improving the product.
  • To prevent abuse, duplicate signups, spam, and security incidents. This is based on our legitimate interests in keeping the site secure and fair.
  • To comply with legal obligations that apply to us.

Where the law requires consent, you can withdraw it at any time by contacting us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened.

4. Cookies, analytics, and third parties

No third-party advertising trackers are enabled by default. First-party analytics are recorded in our own database. The site may support optional PostHog or Plausible analytics, but those integrations are only active if the operator explicitly configures them. If they are enabled, this policy still applies and the provider processes data on our instructions.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers ("processors") who handle data only on our instructions and under appropriate agreements:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) for hosting and storage.
  • GitHub for source-code hosting.
  • Email delivery providers we use to send the signals and alerts you request.
  • Yahoo Finance for delayed market quotes. The site proxies quote requests so your IP address is not sent directly to Yahoo.

We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. International transfers

Our servers are located in the United States. Where data of visitors in the EU, UK, or other regions is transferred there, we rely on appropriate safeguards through our hosting provider, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • Signup and event records, including the associated IP address: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Email correspondence: up to 24 months after our last interaction, unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Server logs: approximately 90 days.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • restrict or object to our processing, or withdraw consent;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

If you live in California, you may also have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to delete it, to correct it, and to not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To exercise any of these rights, email anil.ojblabs@gmail.com and we will respond within the time required by law, generally within 30 days. You can also stop receiving emails by contacting us and asking to be removed.

9. Security

We protect data with HTTPS in transit, access controls on the server, and restricted access to the admin console. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

12. Contact

For any privacy question or request, email anil.ojblabs@gmail.com.
